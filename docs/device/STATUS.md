## Stubbed or not modelled

- The Verilog-A source is not compiled or run (no OpenVAF). Its equations match the .lib by
  construction (`docs/device/MODEL_EQUIVALENCE.md`), but nobody has checked it with a compiler.
- No charge trapping, no endurance, no temperature, no gate leakage, no depolarization beyond the
  series charge balance, no domain-to-domain coupling.
- Device variation only as a gate-referred V_th shift with σ from Ni 2019 (not from domain count).
- No time-to-digital converter, no array-level search-line RC, no write drivers (ideal ±4 V sources),
  no row decoder, no supply noise, no corners (TT only), no MOSFET mismatch.
- MOSFET cards are hand-written stand-ins: absolute currents, capacitances and energies are rough.

## Open decisions

1. **Install OpenVAF-reloaded?** (GPL-3.0, prebuilt Linux binary from
   https://fides.fe.uni-lj.si/openvaf/download). Needed to compile and check
   `device/veriloga/fefet_preisach.va` and to run the model as an OSDI device. Until then the
   ngspice-native .lib is the working model.
2. **FreePDK45 or PTM 45 nm for the MOSFETs?** FreePDK45 (Apache-2.0, PTM-derived) is licence-clean;
   PTM has no stated terms. Either needs your approval to download. Swapping is one `.include` line
   (`models/mosfet/standin45_bsim4.lib` → a file that defines `nmos45` and `pmos45`).
3. **Which device is the calibration target for the TCAM?** v0 uses set C (a 28 nm-class device
   fitted to the Ni 2019 numbers only). If you have, or can get, measured switching data for one
   specific TCAM FeFET (MW vs pulse, retention, disturb), that would replace set C.
4. **Readout choice for the pricer** (see the results): the 2-FeFET NOR match line with
   time-domain counting is the Ni 2019 cell; whether to keep it, or move G4 to a clamped-current or
   charge-domain count line, depends on the Monte Carlo result below.

## Next steps

- **G4: a full FeFET TCAM word in SPICE.** Because the Monte Carlo shows that a NOR match line read in
  the time domain cannot count even in an 8-cell sub-word, G4 should first build a count line that
  clamps the per-cell current (1FeFET1R or 2FeFET-2R, `docs/lit/hw_prior_art.md` §2.1) or a
  charge-domain line, and run the same Monte Carlo on it, before building the 64-cell segment. Keep
  the NOR line for match/miss only (the H4 control). Also: search-line RC for an N-row array, the
  pricer's selective precharge (rows disabled by x), a write scheme that does not put negative stress
  on unselected low-V_th cells, smaller FeFETs (search-line energy), then corners, temperature and
  MOSFET mismatch once a real PDK card is approved.
- **G5: mixed-signal co-simulation with the RTL.** The RTL stand-in TCAM first reads
  `char_v0.csv` (latency, energy, per-count error rates). Later, an ngspice shared-library or
  file-based co-simulation of one SPICE word inside the RTL loop.
- Model: compile the Verilog-A with OpenVAF once approved and run tests 1a-1f on it; add charge
  trapping (P1's long-pulse MW drop) and an endurance model before any endurance claim.

### TCAM word results (set-C FeFET W/L 200/100 nm, stand-in 45 nm MOSFETs, VDD 1.0 V, V_S 1.1 V)

8-cell word, nominal devices (`sim/out/t3b/t3b_result.csv`):

| Mismatches | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discharge time (ps), SL edge → SA output | no flip | 122 | 73 | 56 | 47 | 42 | 38 | 36 | 34 |
| Energy per search, whole word (fJ) | 8.3 | 15.5 | 15.4 | 15.4 | 15.4 | 15.4 | 15.4 | 15.4 | 15.4 |

Energy split at 1 mismatch: 6.9 fJ from VDD (ML precharge and the sense amplifier) and 8.6 fJ from
the search-line supply. The search lines dominate because each FeFET gate is a 0.66 fF FE capacitor
in series with the MOS gate (W/L 200/100 nm is large for a 45 nm cell), plus the CMOS driver. Per cell
this is 1.9 fJ, outside the C8 sanity band (0.19-0.75 fJ, from re-simulations that use smaller devices
and probably count only the match line). A {0, X} pricer query with 6 cared cells costs 13.2 fJ;
an all-X query 0.1 fJ. Stored X cells and searched X cells never discharge the line (checked).

64-cell word (one match line): discharge 625 ps at 1 mismatch, 336 ps at 2, 108 ps at 8, 32 ps at 64;
about 97 fJ per search (1.5 fJ/cell). Run cost per search deck: a few seconds with frozen-domain cells.

Monte Carlo, 8-cell word, 200 samples per count, σ(V_th) = 0.10 V (low) / 0.15 V (high)
(`circuit/tcam/char_v0.csv`):

| Count | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean t (ps) | – | 134 | 76 | 57 | 48 | 42 | 39 | 36 | 34 |
| σ t (ps) | – | 52 | 16 | 7.9 | 4.4 | 3.6 | 2.4 | 2.0 | 1.6 |
| P(misread), empirical | 0 | 0.085 | 0.32 | 0.42 | 0.43 | 0.54 | 0.58 | 0.57 | 0.25 |

**Answer to the top readout risk:** with Ni 2019's threshold spread, this match line and a
time-domain read separate "no mismatch" from "at least one mismatch" without error in 200 samples
(no false flips, no missed mismatches), but it cannot count. Count 1 is read as 2 in 8.5 % of
samples; counts 2-8 are misread 25-58 % of the time. So **one boundary (two levels) can be told
apart**, not nine. The cause is the spread of the one or two low-V_th FeFETs that set the discharge
current: at 1 mismatch the discharge time has σ/mean = 39 %. An extra run at one third of that
spread (σ = 33 / 50 mV, information only, `sim/out/t3d_s033/`) reads counts 0, 1 and 2 with ≤ 1 %
error (three levels), while counts 3-8 are still misread 1.5-11 % of the time (the 1/(m(m+1))
crowding of discharge times). Even a three times tighter device does not make 0-8 counting work
on one match line.

What this means for the pricer: a NOR match line with discharge-time sensing is fine as a match/miss
detector (H4 visited-state control) but not as a mismatch counter. The count readout must clamp the
per-cell current (1FeFET1R, 2FeFET-2R) or move to a charge-domain count line, as
`docs/lit/hw_prior_art.md` §2.4 already suggested; that is the first G4 decision.

### Write scheme

The two-phase write (all gates −4 V, then +4 V on the cells that must be low V_th; selected row's
ML, ScL and well at 0 V) writes the word exactly as a single device (0.1 mV). The V_W/2 inhibit
fails in this model: an unselected low-V_th cell under −2 V for 2.55 ms (one write of each of 255
other rows) loses 0.72 V of window; −1.33 V (V_W/3) still moves it 78 mV. Positive stress and stress
on high-V_th cells are harmless. A real array would need a smaller negative inhibit level, per-row
writes that avoid negative stress on unselected low-V_th cells, or a device with a stronger
negative-field threshold. This needs measured disturb data to settle (decision 3 above).
