# Stage 1b: equal-effort re-tune with effective grids, and the first energy (ETS) numbers (local; tuning-only, ideal device)

Triggered by internal review item 9.3.2 and 9.3.4. Commit `855513e` added continuous `t_hi_mult` / `t_lo_mult` knobs (the percentile knobs are inert because |dE| is integer-valued) and 12 behaviour-distinct configurations per arm (`scripts/testing/s1_retune_grids.py`, copied into `prereg/hw/S1_tuning_grids.json`; verified by `s1b_retune.py check`: 12 -> 12 distinct effective parameter sets for every arm). Edges were widened for every arm including ours (K down to 0.25n, c up to 0.16, T_end multipliers down to 1/16, T_start up to x2.5, kc2c T_end x0.1 / x0.25). ours K = 2n stays as a separate non-selectable reference row (`ours_ref_H8`), as does the H8-registered B3 (`B3_ref_H8`: p90/5, knob 0.5, not representable in the new B3 grid).
Run: 50 jobs, p3n480 tuning instances 2101-2108 x seeds 0-2 (24 runs per configuration), ideal device, tag 10 (tuning only), sequentially through local job queue (4.6 h wall, one job at a time, RSS <= 0.2 GB). Full records were saved so that ETS99 could be computed with the H8 evaluator machinery (`s1b_ets.py`: `evalcommon.arm_data` / `boot_stats`, `MeasuredCostV2` with corrected flags B-1..B-3). Evidence: `s1b_evidence/` (selection, ETS table, JSONL archive, job list, log); full records in `00_admin/s1_return/s1b_retune/records.tar.zst`.
A bug in my selection rule was found and fixed before anything was read into a verdict: the first `select` call also ranked the two reference rows (B3_ref_H8 came out first); the rule now excludes `ref` and `*_ref_H8` rows. Both calls used the same data; only the fixed one is reported.

**Energy risk stated plainly:** ours spends 1.3-1.5x more energy per run than B3, so success parity does not give energy parity; at success 0.83 vs 0.71-0.79 the ETS ratio is about 1, and the nominal condition adds the mitigation energy of ours (H8: +10-21 %) while B3 is digital, which would push the ratio to roughly 1.0-1.8 (a rough scaling of these ideal numbers, not a measured nominal ETS). An ETS loss to B3 at 480 with success parity is therefore a live outcome of Stage 2; the larger kick itself is not the cause.

## 3. Edge probe (done): the edge winners were not beaten
Same rule for every arm whose winner sat on a grid edge: 4 extra configurations beyond the edge, same 8 instances x 3 seeds (16 jobs, 2.6 h wall locally, local job queue one at a time; `s1b_retune.py plan-probe`; every grid + probe set is 16 distinct effective parameter sets): ours K in {0.125, 0.0625} x c in {0, 0.04}; B3 T_final multiplier in {1/32, 1/64} x T_start in {x1.5, x2}; ours_none T_start in {x3, x4} x T_end in {x0.5, x1}; ours_kc2c c in {0.12, 0.16} x K in {1, 0.5}. Selection rule re-run on the 16 configurations of each arm (`s1b_evidence/selection_1a_probe.json`):
| arm | winner before | winner after the probe | best probe configuration |
|---|---|---|---|
| ours_kc2_i11 | c 0, K = 0.25n (0.83) | **unchanged** | c 0, K = 0.0625n: 0.79 (K = 0.125n: 0.75) |
| B3_v2 | T_start x1.5, T_final x0.0625 (0.71) | **unchanged** | T_final x1/32: 0.42, x1/64: 0.25 |
| ours_kc2c | T_end x0.25, K = n, c 0.08 (0.46) | **unchanged** | no probe beats 0.46 |
| ours_none | T_start x2, T_end x0.5 (0.38) | **unchanged** | T_start x3: 0.25 |
So the selected configurations are interior to the probed region for every arm: the optimum is bracketed (success falls on both sides), and the Stage 2 configurations in the draft are the Stage 1b selections without change. (ours: K = 0.25n is the best of a flat region, 0.75-0.83 for K between 0.0625n and 0.5n at c <= 0.04.)
**Selection criterion matters for an energy study.** The registered rule maximises final success. ETS99 takes the minimum over the budget grid, so a fast configuration with lower final success can have a lower ETS: ours (c 0.04, K = 0.125n, success 0.75) has ETS 9.4e11 fJ and ours (c 0, K = 0.0625n, success 0.79) 1.0e12 versus 1.4e12 for the success-selected configuration; B3 (T_final x1/64, success 0.25) has ETS 8.7e11 versus 9.4e11. ETS-selected contrasts (same-data selection, optimistic for every arm alike): ours / B3 = 1.08 [0.21, 2.51] (success +0.50 in ours' favour), ours (ETS-selected) / B3 H8 reference = 0.62 [0.24, 1.47]. Across the comparators and selection rules the ratio runs from 0.6 to 1.5 with every interval containing 1: **energy parity at 480 is not resolved on the tuning data**, and the ETS-selected B3 is an unattractive operating point (success 0.25 at full budget), which a hardware user would not run. The draft therefore keeps the registered success-first rule as primary and reports the ETS-selected variant as a sensitivity.
