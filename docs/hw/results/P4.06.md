Key finding: the filter's error comes from unverified level-0 cells, not from the verified levels. cim_cell_v1 level 0
has sigma 4.86 nA (sigma/mu 3.67) against a 14.16 nA unit; ~1000 empty replica cells plus the empty weight cells sum to an
offset of order 100 units. Write-verify of levels 1-4 alone does not make the HyCiM filter usable on these models; a
fix (verify/deep-erase level 0, gate unused rows, smaller replica) would be a design change and is left to the author.
Caveats: max(0, N) for level 0 is a Gaussian stand-in for a skewed distribution (biases the mean up); comparator offset
(7.4 mV) not mapped to current; write-disturb only as stress ratio (0.70-0.83 of the P1.07 255 x 10 us stress), not
applied; write-verify residual uniform +-5 % (t4a band) is a model; the A3 SA re-run uses the exact check, valid at
sigma = 0 by A1 only. TCAM char_v0 not used (plan specifies a 1FeFET1R matchline filter).
