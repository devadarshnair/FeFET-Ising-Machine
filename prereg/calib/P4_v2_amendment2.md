## A. R4 is re-labelled (no new run): the paper's own definition decides
HyCiM Sec. 3.1 / 3.4 (Fig. 6b): "During each SA iteration, the SA logic generates an input variable configuration"; an infeasible
configuration "initiat[es the] next SA iteration". So one iteration = one proposal (feasible or not). **The registered R4 outcome is
therefore FAIL: 16.46 % per run at 1000 proposals** (the literal-1000 column of the R4 run). The 99.43 % at 1000 sweeps is kept only as
a labelled alternative reading that the paper text contradicts.

## B. R4b: re-attempt under the paper's definition, with the paper's run design and its Fig. 10 scoring unit
Fidelity gaps of R4 against Sec. 4.3, fixed here:
1. Run design: the paper uses **1000 Monte Carlo initial configurations x 100 SA runs per initial configuration**; R4 used 100 x 10.
2. Scoring unit: Fig. 10 plots **1000 points per instance, one per initial state** ("1000 Initial states generated by Monte Carlo
   sampling" on its x-axis), with the normalised QKP value each initial state reached. The only quantity with one value per initial state
   over its 100 runs is the best of those runs. So the Fig. 10 reading is: an initial state succeeds if the best feasible profit of its
   100 runs is >= 0.95 x optimum; the success rate is the mean over initial states and instances.
3. Iteration = one proposal, L = 1000 (paper definition, A above). Move set, schedule and initial-state sampler stay as R4 (v3: S4,
   50 % swap / 50 % flip, feasible Monte Carlo initial states, as the Sec. 4.1 MC sampler generates feasible configurations). These
   are not published; they are not tuned here.
