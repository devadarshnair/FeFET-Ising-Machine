## Root cause of the attempt-1 failure (diagnosis, 16 Nov 2026)
- Not a units/sign/parsing bug: golden objective == G-set cut is asserted per run; SA reaches 99.4/99.0/99.3 % of
  best-known on G48/G49/G50 and 99.9 % on G1-G3.
- MESA arm: the registered schedule is geometric over the WHOLE run (300 sweeps), while the registered restart sets
  T := T0 on every stall (Count_max = 2n). After a reset T falls by only (T_end/T0)^(2/300) before the next stall, so
  MESA stays at T ~ T0 for the entire run (146-148 restarts per 300 sweeps on every graph, one per 2n steps): a hot
  random walk from the best state. Mean final cut = 66-68 % of best-known on G48-G50 (T1 = -32 %), 93 % on G1-G5
  (target 0.99 BKS never reached -> T2 undefined). Regression test: tests/hw/test_calib_b1.py::
  test_attempt1_mesa_restarts_every_count_max_regression.
- The B1 parameter sheet (docs/hw/lit/yin2024_supp.md row 19) specifies "geometric (exponential) cooling PER EPOCH";
  P4.02 registered the whole-run schedule, which is inconsistent with MESA's T := T0 epoch reset (row 18).

## Deviation (MESA arm only; SA rows of attempt 1 reused unchanged)
Per-epoch geometric cooling: within each MESA epoch T falls geometrically from T0 to T_end (the registered Aramon
p85/p05 values) over E = 10 sweeps (10 n steps), then is held at T_end until the stall restart (T := T0, as
registered). Count_max in {n, 4n} as registered for attempt 2. Arms: mesa_k1_e10, mesa_k4_e10.
E is a new unpublished knob ([U]); 10 sweeps chosen a priori (Yin Fig. S7a x-axis ~1200 iterations ~ 1.5 sweeps of
800 nodes is the only published scale; 10 sweeps gives 30 epochs in the 300-sweep budget). Not tuned on scored data.
Everything else (budget, T rule, targets, tolerance, seeds, instances) unchanged.

## T1 is unattainable under the registered SA baseline (stated in advance)
The registered SA baseline already reaches >= 99.0 % of best-known on G48-G50, so any MESA improvement is bounded by
about +1 %; |T1 - 27| <= 10 cannot pass for any MESA variant. Attempt 2 is therefore expected to FAIL T1, and the
P4.02 fail label applies to T1. Yin's +27 % implies an SA baseline far from converged (budget/schedule unpublished).
T2 remains a meaningful test.
