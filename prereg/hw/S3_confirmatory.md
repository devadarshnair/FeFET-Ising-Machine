## 1. Question and decision rule (fixed before data)
Does ours with the exact incremental-field architecture (T-1 with T-5 and T-6, plus the leaky-zero screening it needs, all priced) have an **energy-to-solution advantage over tuned B3** on fresh problems, **with success non-inferior**, at equal tuning? Energy (ETS99) and success are both primary.
- Per cell, comparators B3_sel (selected in 3.1) and B3_h8 (H8-registered). **SUPPORTED** on a cell only if ours_stack **wins against BOTH**; **FALSIFIED** if it **loses to EITHER** (H8 loss class: ratio >= 1 with the CI lower bound above 1); otherwise **"no energy advantage shown"** (never parity).
- **Win** = ETS99 ratio <= 0.95, Holm p < 0.05, ETS CI upper bound < 1, and success difference **not worse than -0.10** (lower CI bound >= -0.10). A comparator at 0 % success is "not evaluable" (EVAL-1 on) and drops out of the dual rule (at least one B3 comparator must be evaluable on the cell); it is still reported. Stage 3.1 shows B3_h8 does not transfer to p4n480 (0 of 24 runs), so on p4n480 the decision rests on B3_sel alone.
- **Why -0.10 (design limit, not a power fix):** the H8 margin of -1 pp requires the success-difference lower bound to be at least -1 pp; with 128-192 runs per cell the CI half-width is about 0.1, so that rule is satisfiable only for a true success gain of about +0.10 or more: it demands superiority in success, whereas the hypothesis is energy with success not materially worse. ETS99 already penalises lower success (ETS ~ energy per run x ln 0.01 / ln(1 - p)): at p = 0.5 a 10-point lower success costs a factor of about 1.2 in ETS, so a "win" at -0.10 must beat that. **The -1 pp rule is reported as a pre-declared sensitivity next to every verdict**; the decision uses -0.10.
- Primary family for Holm: m = 4 cells (p3n480, p4n480, p3n960, Max-Cut) x 2 B3 comparators = **8** (B1, bridge and bit-sliced contrasts are report-only). Fallback if the budget cannot take p3n960: it becomes report-only and m = 6.
- **Hardware-estimate requirement (pre-declared) and T-1 stack parameters:** the T-1 stack is priced with the full pricing (`t1f` + t5 + t6; the engineering notes s13): refresh reads, one-time screening (verify of every level-0 cell, 1 % retired and re-erased), spare rows (2 %), the correction/remap tables with their checks, the field registers (area and leakage). **Primary parameters (updated, pricing 9de93f1): screening only (correction table 0) if it passes the Stage 3.1b rule, otherwise the table-4 stack; refresh period R = 200 (insensitive: R 25-1000 moves the ratio by <= 0.03), spares 2 %, screening threshold chosen in Stage 3.1b by the declared rule (cheapest configuration with success >= 0.87 at the three cliff points).** Pre-declared sensitivities: table 4 (0.76) and table 16 (priced ratio 1.08 on the Stage 2 counters: the lead disappears), screening-only is the expected primary (0.66 [0.43, 0.95]; x2 estimates 0.69), spares 0 / 5 %, screening 3 % retired, and all unmeasured terms (1-bit sense energy, field-register class, t5 controller) at x2 and x0.5. The decision is evaluated at nominal and at x2 on every estimated term; **a "supported" statement is made only if the verdict also holds at x2**, otherwise "supported at nominal estimates only". For orientation (exploratory, stored Stage 2 counters, not a result): primary 0.76 [0.49, 1.10] against B3_sel and 0.64 [0.43, 0.86] against B3_h8 at p3n480; at x2 0.90 [0.58, 1.29]; at x0.5 0.69.
- **Primary stack excludes t2 (sparse storage):** it adds nothing on top of t1 in the screen and is offered to ours only (B3's SRAM would also shrink); t2 and the sparse no-diff column are report-only sensitivities.
